Bandra Residence Revitalized

The Challenge

The original space was compartmentalized, dark, cluttered with outdated textures and materials, and lacked proper circulation space or architectural flow.

Our Solution

We opened up partitions to establish a fluid layout, layered it with warm oak floors, bookmatched Calacatta marble wall cladding, and minimalist cream-toned furniture.

The Result

A bright, elegant room that feels twice as spacious, utilizing warm metallic brass accents to capture a sophisticated, timeless look.
